Plant Care Tips

Simple habits that help your plants thrive.

💧 Do Not Overwater

Overwatering is the #1 cause of houseplant stress. Always check the soil moisture—let the top 1 to 2 inches dry out completely before watering again.

☀️ Choose the Right Light

Most houseplants love bright, indirect light. Direct sunlight can scorch delicate leaves, while dark corners will slow down growth.

🪴 Use Pots with Drainage

Ensure your pots have drainage holes at the bottom. Proper drainage prevents water from sitting at the roots and causing root rot.
